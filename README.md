# Mid-Del Auto gallery template

A responsive, standalone automotive gallery and customer reviews template. No
build step or framework is needed.

## Try it out

Open `index.html` in a recent browser, choose **Add a project**, then enter a
customer, vehicle, service, and both photos. Search and service filters help
find saved projects. Use **Remove project** to delete an entry.

Open `reviews.html` to view, filter, and add customer reviews with ratings.
Gallery projects and reviews are stored in this browser on this device.
Nothing is uploaded to a server. Clearing browser data can remove saved
content, so keep separate copies of customer photos. For a live website,
replace browser-only storage with a secure backend and confirm you have
permission to publish customer photos and reviews.

## Make it yours

The gallery and reviews pages use the official logo from
[mid-delauto.com](https://www.mid-delauto.com/) and its navy, blue, and red
palette. Both header and footer logos link to the local homepage
(`index.html`). Update the contact email, navigation links, and service options
to match the business. The hero artwork is CSS illustration; gallery cards use
the photos you add.

## Files

- `index.html` — page content and add-project form
- `reviews.html` — customer review and rating page
- `assets/mid-del-auto-logo.png` — official Mid-Del Auto logo
- `styles.css` — responsive layout and visual design
- `app.js` — local photo storage, search, filters, and project management
- `reviews.js` — local reviews, ratings, and filters
